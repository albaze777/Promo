2. `bun run audio` (writes `public/audio/promo.wav`). Check the loudness with ffmpeg `ebur128`: about −17 LUFS.
3. **Quick check before the long render:** render stills of the heaviest and newest scenes and look at them:
   `bun scripts/render.ts stills --t 3.6,7,9.5,20.8,40,46.85,58.5,74,79,85.6,94 --out out/stills/check`
   A scene that throws renders **dark red**: look for that. In the browser log, a 404 is only the favicon.
4. **Measure the cost first** (the detail work made frames slower; the 4-sub-frame timings measured last session were dino ≈ 9.4 s/frame, map ≈ 8.1 s, wheel ≈ 7.3 s). Time 3 consecutive frames of a few scenes with `--samples 4` and use the later frames, because the first includes shader compilation. From that, decide the settings:
   - preferred: 60 fps, `--samples 4`, shutter 0.5, CRF 16;
   - if the projected time is unreasonable, fall back to 30 fps `--samples 4`, and say so.

   Write down which you chose and why.
5. **Render** with the resumable chunk renderer, detached:
   `setsid nohup scripts/run_claim.sh out/segments_v3 out/oredlab-promo_master3.mp4 2 --fps 60 --samples 4 --shutter 0.5 --crf 16 --preset slow --chunk 5 > /dev/null 2>&1 &`

   It starts two renderer processes sharing a queue of 5 s chunks; finished chunks are skipped if restarted. When `out/logs/concat.log` ends with `DONE`, it has joined the chunks and added the audio. Report progress from the logs. Don't run other renders or stills while it runs (they compete for CPU).
6. **Verify:**
   - `ffprobe -count_frames` on the master must report **5738 video frames** (at 60 fps) and about 95.6 s of audio.

7. **Encode** with `scripts/encode_delivery.sh oredlab-promo_master3.mp4` (paths are relative to `out/`). It produces:
   - `out/repo.mp4`: two-pass 7.8 Mbps; must be < 100 MB;
   - `out/share1080.mp4`: 2.3 Mbps; must be < 30 MiB = 31,457,280 bytes;
   - `out/share720.mp4`.

   Check the sizes; if one is over its limit, lower that bitrate and re-encode. Copy the files to `renders/oredlab-promo.mp4` and `renders/oredlab-promo_share.mp4`.
8. Update the render notes in `README.md` and `docs/HANDOFF.md` (settings used, measured times, file sizes, frame count). Commit and push.

## 3. Gotchas (all hit before; details in docs/HANDOFF.md)

- Stop renders with `kill -9` on exact PIDs from `ps -eo pid,args`. `pkill -f` / `pgrep -f` also match your own shell. Kill the orphaned `chrome` and `vite` processes too.
- Two renderer workers inside one process jam each other's browser; the scripts already use separate processes.
- `render.ts stills --only <ids>` loads only those timeline entries. The panel scenes (dino 05a, wheel 05b, AI 05c, infinite 05d) live inside `digital`, and 7–9.5 s needs `world,evo`. A wrong `--only` gives a red or empty frame. That is a usage mistake, not a bug.
- Writable disk is limited. Delete old chunk folders and stills you no longer need.
